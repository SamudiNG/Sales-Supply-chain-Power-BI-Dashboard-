# Sales & Supply Chain Performance Dashboard

##  Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales and supply chain performance. The dashboard provides insights into sales, profitability, order performance, shipping efficiency and product returns to support data driven decision making.

## Business Problem 

Retail businesses need to monitor sales performance alongside operational indicators such as shipping duration and product returns.
Limited visibility into these metrics can make it difficult to identify underperforming areas, evaluate shipping efficiency and understand differences in performance across regions and product categories.
This project uses retail order data to explore these business challenges through an interactive analytical dashboard.

##  Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard includes analysis of,

- Monthly sales trends
- Sales and profit by product category
- Regional sales performance
- Top 10 products by sales
- Average shipping duration by shipping mode
- Returned orders by product category
- Return rates across categories
- Shipping performance against a five-day benchmark


##  Key Insights


- Technologygenerated the highest sales (0.84M) and profit (145K).
- The West region recorded the highest sales (0.73M), followed by the East region (0.68M).
- Office Supplies recorded the highest number of returned orders (234).
- Technology had the highest return rate (7.97%), followed by Furniture (7.71%) and Office Supplies (6.25%).
- Standard Class had the longest average shipping duration (42 days), while Same Day shipping had the shortest (1 day).
- 61.99% of orders met the selected five-day shipping benchmark. This threshold is an analytical assumption, not a verified company policy.
  
## Business Recommendations 

- Investigate Shipping Delays- Review orders with unusually long shipping durations, particularly Standard Class shipments and check for potential data-quality      issues.
- Analyze Product Returns- Investigate the reasons behind the high number of returned orders in Office Supplies and the highest return rate in Technology.
- Improve Regional Performance- Examine factors contributing to stronger sales in the West and East regions and identify potential opportunities in lower-           performing regions.
- Monitor Category Profitability- Track category-level profitability and investigate why Furniture generates comparatively lower profit despite significant sales.
- Track Shipping Efficiency- Regularly monitor shipping performance against the selected five-day analytical benchmark to identify orders requiring further          investigation.

## Methodology & Assumptions 

- Data Preparation- Used Excel and Power Query to prepare and transform the retail order dataset.
- Data Modeling- Organized the data in Power BI to support sales and supply chain analysis.
- KPI Development- Used DAX measures and calculated columns to calculate sales, profit, order returns and shipping performance.
- Shipping Duration- Calculated the number of days between the order date and the ship date.
- Five-Day Shipping Benchmark- Selected five days as an analytical threshold to evaluate shipping performance. This is an assumption made for the project and does   not represent a verified company shipping policy.
- Shipping Interpretation- The shipping benchmark measures time from order placement to shipment, not confirmed delivery to the customer.
- Analytical Limitations- Unusually long shipping durations require further investigation to determine whether they reflect operational issues, data-quality         problems or other factors.
